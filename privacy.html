What we collect

Only the details you choose to give us in the private request form:

  • Property details — address, or just city / ZIP if you prefer, home type, bedrooms, and condition.
  • Your timeline — including “just exploring, no rush at all.”
  • How to reach you — only the email address or phone number you give us, and only in the way you chose: email only, text only, or either.
  • Anything else you choose to add in the notes box.

We do not collect anything else about you, and we do not make a public listing of your home.

Why we collect it

To prepare one private, firm offer for your home and to reply to you only in the way you chose. That is all it is used for. There is no obligation, and no listing is made.

What we do not do with your details

  • We do not sell your details.
  • We do not share your details with other buyers.
  • We do not call you unless you ask for a call.
  • We do not put a sign in your yard, hold showings, or list your home publicly.

How long we keep your details

We keep your request only as long as needed to prepare your offer and answer you, and then to keep a record of that offer if you ask us to. You can ask us to delete your details at any time — see below — and we will delete them unless we have to keep a copy for a legal reason, in which case we will tell you.

Seeing or deleting your details
